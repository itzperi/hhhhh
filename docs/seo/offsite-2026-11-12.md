## 4. Review routine (no incentives, ask everyone)

**Script (front desk, after the visit):** "If you were happy with your visit, would you share it on Google? Here is a card with the link. Your honest opinion helps other families find us. Thank you either way."

Rules: ask every patient, not only happy ones; never offer a gift, discount or anything else; never ask for a rating of a given number; never post a review on a patient's behalf.

**Four reply templates** that never confirm someone is a patient (privacy law forbids it):

1. **Positive:** "Thank you for taking the time to write this. We are glad to hear it. If you ever have questions, please call us at 916-236-5800."
2. **Negative about service:** "We are sorry that your experience fell short. We take this seriously and would like to learn more. Please call 916-236-5800 and ask for the office manager."
3. **Mentions clinical care:** "Thank you for your feedback. We cannot discuss any individual's care in a public reply. Please call 916-236-5800 so we can talk privately."
4. **Wrong place or suite:** "Thank you for writing. Apollo Physicians is at 8191 Timberlake Way, Suite 200. If this review was meant for another practice, please let us know. Call 916-236-5800."
