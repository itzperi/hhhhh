# Baseline audit: apollophysicians.com (Phase 1 and Phase 0)

12 Nov 2026. Read-only audit of the live site. Specialist reports are in `audits-2026-11-12/` (GEO, schema, technical). Scores are the auditors' judgement from on-site evidence, not measured rankings. **Not measured:** Core Web Vitals (PageSpeed quota), Search Console data, Google Rich Results Test, real AI-assistant answers, off-site profiles on Google Business Profile, Bing and Apple.

How the audit ran: the Full audit of `/seo-geo-aeo` (content and answer-engine crawl of all 7 live pages), plus the `seo-geo`, `seo-schema` and `seo-technical` specialists (curl and a parser; no Screaming Frog or Lighthouse). `technical-seo-audit` needs a crawl export, which was not provided, so the fallbacks named in the plan were used. `seo-images` was covered inside the technical audit (the site has one image).

## What is working

- `robots.txt` allows all and names the AI crawlers; `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` match the live pages word for word (55 of 55 answers).
- Name, address (Suite 200), phone and hours are identical in the HTML, the JSON-LD and the llms files.
- Server-rendered HTML (120 KB raw, 15.6 KB transferred), self-canonical on every page, a real 404 status, no stray `noindex`, no staging subdomains besides riolabz.
- No `aggregateRating`, no invented properties, no fax number published.
