# Apollo Physicians: more keywords, answer-engine questions and AI visibility that bring in patients

11 Nov 2026. This builds on the 11 Nov strategy and the comparison in `strategy-2026-11-11-reconciliation.md`. It lists only what is **new**: search terms, answer-engine (AEO) questions and AI-answer (GEO) work the site does not cover yet. Everything is ranked by how directly it brings in a **booked patient**.

## How to read this

- **No search volumes.** No keyword tool is connected. "Value" and "Winnable" are judgements from what appeared in Google on 11 Nov 2026 (`[ESTIMATE]`). Check real demand in Search Console 4 to 8 weeks after launch.
- **Status** says what can ship:
  - **Now**: uses confirmed facts; can go on a live page today.
  - **Held**: the copy exists or can be written; it is waiting for Dr. Panesar's clinical review.
  - **Needs fact**: the owner must answer first (numbered as in `client-confirmations.md`).
- The site rule does not change: publish only what is marked Confirmed.

## 4. AI visibility (GEO): what makes an AI recommend Apollo Physicians

AI assistants answer "find me a doctor" questions by pulling from directories, government lists and sites that state facts plainly. On 11 Nov, Medicare and Hill Physicians searches returned **Zocdoc and Hill Physicians doctor pages**, and immigration searches returned **easyIME and USCIS**. Those are the sources to fix first.

## 5. Measuring patients, not rankings

No analytics vendor is installed (`analytics-decision.md`). Without collecting any health information:

1. **The front desk asks every new patient "How did you hear about us?"** and logs one of: Google search, Google Maps, AI assistant, insurance directory, USCIS list, friend, other. This is the measure that matters.
2. Business Profile Insights: calls, website clicks and direction requests each month.
3. Search Console: impressions and clicks for each search term in section 2, starting 4 weeks after launch.
4. Track immigration exams and self-pay visits separately. They are the highest-value patients this plan targets.
